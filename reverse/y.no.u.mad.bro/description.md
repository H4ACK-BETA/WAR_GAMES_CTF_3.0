There's a terminal still running on a dead subnet. No one remembers
who put it there. No one was supposed to find it.

The operator — some guy named **Jaswanth** — has been alone in there
for what feels like forever. He left messages. He left puzzles. He
left a door open, maybe hoping someone would walk through it.

> "I built this place for someone to find. I just... didn't think
> it would take this long."

There's a phrase he whispers to himself in the dark. A message board
he set up that no one ever posts to. And a signal echo he runs just
to hear something — anything — come back.

Find his terminal. Hear him out. Then take what he's been guarding
all this time. He won't stop you. Honestly... he's been waiting.
