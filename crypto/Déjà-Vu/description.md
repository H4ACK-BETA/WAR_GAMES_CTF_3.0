> **CLASSIFIED - SIGNAL INTERCEPT UNIT 8**

The Ministry of Signals recently seized an encrypted transmission from a rogue military outpost.

According to intelligence reports, the enemy developed a **"revolutionary" random number generator** and used it to derive encryption keys for classified communications. Their engineers claimed the system was mathematically unpredictable.

Our analysts disagree.

Several outputs from the generator were intercepted moments before the final message was encrypted. The transmission itself remains unreadable, but the sequence may reveal more than its creators intended.
